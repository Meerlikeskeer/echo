## WEB-020 — First general-purpose Echo device MVP

**Status:** Implementation complete — verified and independently reviewed; physical acceptance pending · **Branch:** `feat/WEB-020-device-mvp`

**Acceptance:** XIAO ESP32S3 Sense records meetings, conversations, lectures or other
sessions independently to microSD with explicit start/stop, ordered overlapping
WAV chunks, a two-hour bound, visible faults and partial recovery. A calm local
console controls recording and lists/exports sessions. BOOT starts/stops without
a Mac connection. A downloaded `.echo` session is validated and imported into the
existing browser queue for transcription and general-purpose notes. Reimports
preserve identity, incomplete captures stay partial, and no audio is deleted.
Firmware build, targeted tests, required repository checks and independent review
have recorded outcomes. Owner performs physical upload and acceptance.

### Scope and decisions

Base: `main` at 1820c6e. Owner explicitly requested the device MVP and confirmed
microSD availability, then clarified that the product is not lecture-specific.
ADR-023 records the hardware expansion, transfer format, no-deletion policy and
provider boundary. No native Mac receiver, device provider credentials, OTA,
home Wi-Fi provisioning or on-device AI. Download, switch Wi-Fi, import is the
first transfer workflow; it does not claim automatic wireless sync.

One change was made after the recovery. `captureTask` ran on an 8 KiB stack while `hashFile`
alone puts a 2 KiB buffer on it, above the SD/FatFs and mbedtls call depth — thin enough to
crash mid-recording for a reason no host suite can see. It is now 16 KiB, and rather than
substitute a new guess, `GET /api/recording` reports `uxTaskGetStackHighWaterMark` as
`stackFree`, so the first long physical run measures it.
