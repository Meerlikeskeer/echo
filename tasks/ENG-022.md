### Follow-on direction requested by the owner

`PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` already specifies Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) control and
Wi-Fi audio/data transfer as future work. ADR-023 deliberately implements manual
export/import only; there is no automatic handoff or native receiver today.

After website acceptance, open a separate product task for completed-chunk sync
while capture continues, with offline retry, stable identity/checksum validation,
explicit completion evidence, and no raw-audio deletion. Only the final chunk
should remain to process at stop under a healthy connection; latency must be
measured, not promised. A whole-session export after stopping cannot meet this goal.

Connection design and receiver platform must be settled in that task. Prefer
investigating a shared Wi-Fi network before accessory-network switching. ESP32-S3
has a shared 2.4 GHz radio with time-shared BLE/Wi-Fi, not two independent radios:
https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/v5.1/esp32s3/api-guides/coexist.html
Apple's native accessory Wi-Fi join API prompts for permission; this does not
establish browser support or automatic dual-network connectivity:
https://developer.apple.com/documentation/networkextension/nehotspotconfigurationmanager/apply(_:completionhandler:)
The Meta glasses analogy is a desired experience, not an established Echo protocol.
