# WEB-016 — Lecture-length sessions

**Branch:** `feat/WEB-016-long-sessions` · **Base:** `main` @ `5b47990`

Opened because the recorder cannot record a lecture. Three separate limits stop it, and each one is in a different file:

1. `apps/web/src/app/recorder.tsx` stops capture after 60 seconds on a timer.
2. `validateCaptureOptions` in `session.ts` refuses a chunk longer than 60 seconds, and `isAudioChunk` — which is also the upload boundary's validator — refuses chunk metadata spanning more than 120 seconds.
3. The 64 MiB local audio budget holds about 40 minutes at the bitrate browsers pick on their own.

## What this task changes

- Five-minute chunks with five seconds of overlap, chosen by the owner. No auto-stop; a four-hour runaway bound replaces it.
- An explicit Opus bitrate, so a lecture fits the budget and each chunk stays under the upload limit.
- A measured clamp: the effective bitrate is read from the recorder before capture starts, and chunk length is shortened if that bitrate would push a chunk past the upload limit. Safari has been measured at 204 kbps, which would make a five-minute chunk 7.6 MB — rejected by every upload, for the whole lecture, with no way to recover the audio.
- A screen wake lock while recording.

## What it does not change

Stitching, the queue's persistence rules, the job ledger, the notes pipeline. The notes side of a long lecture — the 24,000 character cap that truncates it — is WEB-017, on its own branch.

## Known consequence, accepted

Overlap deduplication in `stitching.ts` matches whole segments with identical timing. Two separate transcription calls almost never produce identical segment boundaries for the same speech, so the five seconds of overlap will usually appear twice in the stitched transcript rather than being merged. WEB-006 chose visible repetition over dropped speech deliberately. At five-minute chunks that is roughly one repeated sentence every five minutes, which the synthesis pass absorbs. A text-similarity merge is worth its own task if it proves annoying in a real transcript.
