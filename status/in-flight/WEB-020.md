# WEB-020 — First device recording MVP

Branch: `feat/WEB-020-device-mvp`, based on `origin/main` (1820c6e at start).

Owner requested the first usable Echo firmware on XIAO ESP32S3 Sense and will flash
and physically test it. Scope: persistent microSD general-session recording, local
recording-first console, recovery and export, and a path into Echo's existing
transcript/notes processing without device provider keys. This explicitly extends the
former diagnostics-only hardware exception. General meetings/conversations/lectures,
not a lecture-specific product; long sessions are the priority.

## What exists

Contract `ECHO0001` + uint32 LE manifest length + JSON `{version,sessionId,complete,chunks}`
+ ordered WAV bytes. Firmware: `echo-device/include/recorder.h` (capture task, SHA-256
journal, readback verification, completion marker, export, BOOT button),
`recording_format.h` (WAV framing + journal validation, host-testable),
`recording_page.h` (recording-first page at `/`; the diagnostics console moved to
`/diagnostics`). Web: `echo-web-app/src/lib/recording/device-import.ts` and its UI.

Firmware version is `0.2.0`. Both sides of the `.echo` contract were read against each
other field by field — header, little-endian length, the nine chunk fields, WAV framing
and the `size == 44 + (endMs - startMs) * 32` relation all agree.

## Changed after the recovery

The capture task's stack was **8192** bytes while `hashFile` alone places a 2 KiB buffer
on it, above the SD/FatFs and mbedtls call depth — thin enough to be a plausible
first-test crash. Raised to **16384** (RAM is only 21.9% used), and the task now reports
`uxTaskGetStackHighWaterMark` as `stackFree` on `GET /api/recording`, updated per
committed chunk and at task end. **The point is to replace the guess with a
measurement**: the acceptance script has the owner read it after a long run. Rebuilt
after the change, still exit 0.
